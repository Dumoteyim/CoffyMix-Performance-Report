````markdown
# CoffyMix Sales Performance Analytics

![CoffyMix Sales Dashboard](./assets/coffymix-dashboard.png)

##  Project Overview

**CoffyMix Sales Performance Analytics** is an end-to-end Business Intelligence project focused on transforming coffee shop sales data into meaningful business insights.

The project explores **sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, geographic trends, payment methods, operational performance, and profitability** through an interactive analytics dashboard.

The objective is not simply to report sales, but to demonstrate how data can be transformed into **actionable business recommendations**.

---

## Business Problem

CoffyMix generates a significant volume of sales transactions, but raw transaction data alone does not provide management with a clear understanding of:

- What is driving revenue?
- Which products perform best?
- Which cities generate the most sales?
- Who are the most valuable customers?
- What is the repeat customer rate?
- Which customer demographics contribute most to revenue?
- Which payment methods are most commonly used?
- When are sales strongest?
- How are baristas performing?
- Is revenue growth translating into healthy profitability?

The project addresses these questions by bringing multiple business dimensions into a single analytical solution.

---

##  Project Objectives

The key objectives of the project are to:

1. Measure overall sales and revenue performance.
2. Analyze customer purchasing and retention behavior.
3. Identify top-performing products and categories.
4. Evaluate performance across cities.
5. Analyze payment-method preferences.
6. Examine customer demographics.
7. Understand sales patterns by day and time.
8. Evaluate operational performance.
9. Assess product profitability.
10. Generate actionable recommendations for management.

#  Profitability Analysis

One of the most important findings from the project is the gap between **sales growth and profitability**.

The dashboard reports:

- Product Revenue: **₦14.22M**
- Product Profit: **₦3.19M**
- Profit Margin: **22.42%**

The profit margin KPI is identified as **below target**.

This creates a critical management question:

> **How can CoffyMix convert its existing sales growth and customer loyalty into stronger profitability?**

Potential areas for further investigation include:

- Product-level margins
- Ingredient costs
- Supplier pricing
- Product waste
- Pricing strategy
- Discounts
- Portion sizes
- Low-margin products
- Store-level operating costs

#  Key Business Insights

### 1. Sales are growing

CoffyMix reports **9.6% sales/revenue growth**, indicating positive commercial momentum.

### 2. Customer loyalty is strong

A **76.85% repeat customer rate** suggests that customer retention is a significant business strength.

### 3. Lagos is the leading market

Lagos generates approximately **₦5.5M**, making it the strongest city in the analysis.

### 4. Revenue is diversified across categories

Although coffee is the largest category, bakery and cold drinks contribute significant revenue.

### 5. Beverage products are powerful revenue drivers

Smoothie, Milkshake and Frappuccino rank among the strongest individual products.

### 6. Profitability requires attention

The **22.42% profit margin** is below target, suggesting that increasing revenue alone may not be sufficient.

### 7. Weekday demand is important

The day-of-week analysis indicates that CoffyMix experiences strong weekday demand, creating opportunities for demand-based operational planning.

---

#  Recommendations

## 1. Improve Product-Level Profitability

Calculate revenue, cost, profit and margin for every product to identify high-value and low-margin products.

## 2. Strengthen Customer Loyalty

Build targeted loyalty initiatives around the existing repeat customer base.

## 3. Increase Average Order Value

Introduce strategic bundles such as:

- Coffee + Bakery
- Smoothie + Snack
- Milkshake + Bakery
- Frappuccino + Pastry

## 4. Optimize Promotions

Use targeted rather than broad discounts to avoid unnecessarily reducing margins.

## 5. Investigate the Lagos Success Model

Identify the customer, product, pricing and operational factors responsible for Lagos's strong performance.

## 6. Optimize Staffing

Align staffing levels and employee schedules with observed demand patterns.

## 7. Expand the Profitability Dashboard

Future versions should incorporate:

- Cost of goods sold
- Gross profit
- Gross margin %
- Store-level profit
- Product-level margin
- Customer acquisition cost
- Customer lifetime value
- Inventory waste
- Sales per employee

#  Analytical Approach

The project follows an end-to-end analytics mindset:

```text
Business Problem
       ↓
Data Collection
       ↓
Data Preparation
       ↓
Data Modeling
       ↓
KPI Development
       ↓
Exploratory Analysis
       ↓
Dashboard Development
       ↓
Insight Generation
       ↓
Business Recommendations
       ↓
Decision Making
````

The goal is to move beyond:

> **"What happened?"**

toward:

> **"Why did it happen?"**

and ultimately:
